📜 The rules — exactly as the contract enforces them

  1. Who can play. Your wallet must hold a Binance .ai Agent ID (the SPACE ID .ai NFT at 0x60B9262456dE011F7d15a54CdD8c48bAaa8B022F). The contract checks this on every mint.
  2. One pass per wallet. A wallet can mint at most one Crown Race Pass, ever, and can hold at most one at a time. Total supply is capped at 2,222.
  3. Free first, then paid. The first 500 passes are minted with mint() at value exactly 0 (gas only). Once they are gone, passes are minted with mintPaid() at exactly 0.0007 BNB. There is no paid mint while free passes remain, and no free mint after they run out.
  4. Mint must be open. Minting works only while the owner has the mint open (mintOpen() = true). Prices are constants in the contract — nobody can change them after deployment.
  5. Boosts. Only pass holders can boost. Tier 0 costs exactly 0.00015 BNB for +5 points, tier 1 exactly 0.0005 BNB for +20, tier 2 exactly 0.0015 BNB for +75. Boosts can be repeated without limit, and the full amount of every boost enters that day's pool.
  6. Days are UTC days. Points are counted per UTC day and a day ends at 00:00 UTC (07:00 WIB). Points do not carry over.
  7. The lead. The leader is the wallet with the most points today. On a tie, the wallet that reached the tied score first keeps the lead — a later equal score does not take it.
  8. Settling. After a day has ended, anyone can call settleDay() — no owner needed. It settles the oldest unsettled day: the day's leader is recorded as its king (kingOfDay, crownsWon +1) and is paid 50% of that day's boost pool; the other 50% goes to the project owner. A day with no boosts settles with no winner and no payment.
  9. No approvals, ever. Minting and boosting never ask for token approvals. If anything asks you to approve a token spend for this race, it is not this contract — stop.
  10. No promises. Points have no monetary value. The only payout that exists is the winner's 50% pool share described above, paid by the contract at settle. A win is never guaranteed — it depends entirely on the day's competition.
